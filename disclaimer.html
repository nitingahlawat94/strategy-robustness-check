What a report can and cannot tell you

  • Reports are estimates based on simulations and statistical rules. The simulations use random sampling. A fixed seed makes them repeatable, but they are still approximations.
  • We treat trades as independent. Real markets have streaks, regimes and crashes that this assumption can understate.
  • We cannot know how many versions of a strategy you tried unless you tell us. Telling us less than the truth makes the results look better than they are.
  • Small samples give unreliable answers. Reports with under 100 trades are capped for this reason.
  • The 0 to 100 score is a judgment-based summary, and its weights are described on the methodology page. Read the warnings before the score.
  • Safe-size figures, drawdown odds and similar numbers are scenarios, not forecasts or advice.
