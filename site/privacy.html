Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

The short version

  • Your trade files are read and analysed inside your browser. They are not uploaded to us.
  • Sign in is required to associate each purchased report with your account. We have no ads, no tracking cookies and no analytics today.
  • We only receive information you choose to send us: an email, a certificate request, or a review.
  • Approved reviews and any certificates actually issued are public by design. No customer certificates have been issued yet.

What stays on your device

When you load a trade file, the analysis runs locally using code that was downloaded with the page. The file contents are not sent anywhere. After a report finishes, your browser saves a short history entry in its local storage so you can see your past reports: the Report ID, score, number of trades, the file name, and the date. This stays on your device. You can remove it any time with the "Clear saved reports" button in the tool, or by clearing your browser's site data.

What we receive, and only if you send it

  • Emails and messages: your name, email address and whatever you write. We use it to reply.
  • Certificate requests: the account stores the Report ID, score, trade count, requested holder name and request status. The trade file is not stored in the account database. If you email it to support for a future manual review, it will be handled only for that review and deleted after the decision. No customer certificates have been issued yet.
  • Reviews: the rating, text and optional display name you type, stored in our database. Please do not include personal details.

Accounts, saved reports and payments

Sign-in uses your email and any OAuth provider that has been configured. We store your email address and account session. You may optionally save your name and mobile number in your private account profile. Planned report prices are $7 for Basic and $23 for Pro, one-time per report, but checkout is currently disabled; no live payment is being accepted. When a payment provider is configured, we will store the payment status, amount, currency, report ID, package and account reference, but not card details. Report summaries may store the Report ID, score, headline, trade count and source name. The analysis file and individual trades remain on your device and are not stored in your account.

What is public

If a real certificate is issued in the future, its signed public registry entry will show the Report ID, score, headline, trade count, issue date, requested holder name (if any), and verification level. The current registry contains a clearly labeled synthetic demo only; no customer certificate has been issued. Approved reviews may show the rating, text and display name. We will remove an entry or review on request where possible.

Third-party services

  • Cloudflare Workers hosts this website. Like any host, it receives your IP address and basic request details in its server logs. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Cloudflare cdnjs serves a spreadsheet reader, and your browser loads it only when you choose an Excel file.
  • Supabase provides our database and sign-in, and stores accounts, report entitlements, saved report summaries, certificate requests and reviews.
  • Razorpay is the planned payment provider; checkout is not active yet.
  • jsDelivr serves the account library, and your browser loads it only when accounts are switched on.
  • Gmail or your own email provider carries messages when you contact us.

Cookies and analytics

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies and do not run analytics today. If you sign in, your browser stores a sign-in session token in its local storage so you stay signed in. If we add privacy-friendly analytics later, we will update this page first, and it will never include your trade data.

How long we keep things

Trade files for certificate requests: deleted within 30 days of our decision. Emails: kept for as long as needed to help you and to keep records. Certificate registry entries and approved reviews: kept until you ask us to remove them. Account data and saved report summaries: kept until you delete them or ask us to delete your account.

Your rights

You can ask us to tell you what personal data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop using it where we rely on your consent. We aim to handle personal data in line with applicable law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. We aim to answer requests within 30 days.

Children

Robustly is for adults. It is not meant for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Security

The best protection for your trade data is that we never receive it. Even so, no website or email system is perfectly secure, and your device and browser are your responsibility. Do not send us passwords or broker login details. We will never ask for them.

Where data may be handled

Our hosting and email providers may process data on servers outside India.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle data, we will update this page and its date, and make any new data collection opt-in.
