Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

The short version

  • Your trade files are read and analysed inside your browser. They are not uploaded to us.
  • We have no accounts, no ads, no tracking cookies and no analytics today.
  • We only receive information you choose to send us: an email, a certificate request, or a review.
  • Certificates and approved reviews are public by design.

What stays on your device

When you load a trade file, the analysis runs locally using code that was downloaded with the page. The file contents are not sent anywhere. After a report finishes, your browser saves a short history entry in its local storage so you can see your past reports: the Report ID, score, number of trades, the file name, and the date. This stays on your device. You can remove it any time with the "Clear saved reports" button in the tool, or by clearing your browser's site data.

What we receive, and only if you send it

  • Emails and messages: your name, email address and whatever you write. We use it to reply.
  • Certificate requests: you email us your Report ID and attach the trade file so we can reproduce the report before signing. We use the file only for that purpose and delete it within 30 days after we issue or decline the certificate.
  • Reviews: the rating, text and optional display name you type, sent to a third-party form service. Please do not include personal details.

What is public

A signed certificate is listed in a public registry. It shows the Report ID, score, headline, number of trades, issue date, the name you asked us to print (if any), and the certificate level. Approved reviews are shown with the rating, text and display name. Once something is public, others may have copied it, so we cannot guarantee that removing it from our site removes every copy. We will remove a registry entry or review on request.

Third-party services

  • GitHub Pages hosts this website. Like any host, it receives your IP address and basic request details in its server logs. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Cloudflare cdnjs serves a spreadsheet reader, and your browser loads it only when you choose an Excel file.
  • A form service (Formspree) receives review submissions, only if we have switched reviews on.
  • Gmail or your own email provider carries messages when you contact us.

Cookies and analytics

We do not use cookies and do not run analytics today. If we add privacy-friendly analytics later, we will update this page first, and it will never include your trade data.

How long we keep things

Trade files for certificate requests: deleted within 30 days of our decision. Emails: kept for as long as needed to help you and to keep records. Certificate registry entries and approved reviews: kept until you ask us to remove them.

Your rights

You can ask us to tell you what personal data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop using it where we rely on your consent. We aim to handle personal data in line with applicable law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. We aim to answer requests within 30 days.

Children

Robustly is for adults. It is not meant for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Security

The best protection for your trade data is that we never receive it. Even so, no website or email system is perfectly secure, and your device and browser are your responsibility. Do not send us passwords or broker login details. We will never ask for them.

Where data may be handled

Our hosting and email providers may process data on servers outside India.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle data, we will update this page and its date, and make any new data collection opt-in.
