        How the score works

        Score out of 100: 20 points for how often profit survives 2,000 resamples of your trades; 15 for profit factor (full marks at 2.0); 15 for sample size (full marks at 200 trades); 20 for Deflated Sharpe confidence; 15 for how close your backtest drawdown is to the 95th-percentile reshuffled drawdown; 15 for staying profitable after removing the best 5% of winners. Up to 15 points are deducted if the second half of your history is much weaker than the first, and up to 25 if your out-of-sample trades fail.

        Limits: scores are capped at 60 when there are fewer than 100 trades. It only sees your trade list, so it cannot know how many variations you tried before this one. It assumes trades are independent, which real markets often violate. The weights are a judgment call, not a proven predictor.

        Deflated Sharpe follows Bailey and López de Prado and assumes your tried variants had noise-like Sharpe ratios, so enter how many variants you tested. Cost stress subtracts a share of your average trade from every trade.

        Luck test: flips the sign of each trade at random 2,000 times to see how often pure chance matches your profit. Scores are capped at 70 above p=0.05 and at 55 above p=0.10. The price walk-forward uses a moving-average demo engine; for your own EA, use the MT5 tester's forward-period option and load the forward trades as out-of-sample.

        Imports are detected automatically from MetaTrader 4/5 statements, TradingView trade lists, broker P&L reports with a realised P&L column (such as Groww's), tradebooks with buy and sell fills (matched first-in-first-out, fees not included) and balance-curve exports. If detection fails you can choose the profit column. Live broker connections are not possible in a static page.

        Analysis of past trades only. It is not investment advice, and a high score does not predict future profit.
