Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Free tool

The checking tool is free. There is nothing to refund or cancel.

Paid plans

Paid plans are described, with their price, at the point of purchase. You can stop using a paid plan at any time. We do not take further payments unless you buy again, and your access continues until the end of the period you paid for.

Refunds

  • If you are not satisfied with a paid plan, email us within 7 days of your payment and we will refund it in full.
  • If you were charged twice or charged the wrong amount, we will refund the difference.
  • If we decline or cannot complete a certificate you paid for, we will refund that fee in full.
  • After 7 days, payments for a period you have already used are not refundable, unless the law says otherwise.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method. Banks and payment providers usually take 5 to 10 business days to show the money.

How to ask

Email with the email you paid with and the date of payment.
