About Robustly

Most strategies look brilliant in a backtest. Few survive real markets. Robustly is built to tell the difference before it costs you money.

What we are building

Robustly stress-tests a trading strategy from the trades it produced. It estimates your worst-case drawdown, your chance of ruin, whether the profit survives costs, whether luck alone could explain it, and whether live trading still matches the backtest. Every report carries an ID that anyone can reproduce, and signed certificates let others verify a result is genuine and unedited.

What we believe

  • A strategy must earn your trust with evidence before it earns a rupee of your capital.
  • Honest beats flattering. A modest true score is worth more than an impressive fake one.
  • Your data is yours. Analysis runs on your device.
  • Every claim needs a test. We publish how the numbers are made, and we test them against strategies where we know the truth.

He is building Robustly in the open, one release at a time, and listens closely to the traders who use it.
